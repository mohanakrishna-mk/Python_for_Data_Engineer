# Modern Data Stack Ecosystem: Highest Demand Skills (Bangalore)

A significant majority of mid-to-senior level postings from Bangalore’s **Global Capability Centers (GCCs)** and **product startups** prioritize candidate alignment around these platforms:

### 1. Databricks & PySpark Mastery
This is the single most heavily repeated keyword combination this month. Recruiters are looking for hands-on experience deploying:
* **Delta Lake Architectures:** Building robust, transactional lakehouse layers.
* **Unity Catalog:** Implementing enterprise-grade data governance, access controls, and lineage tracking.
* **Optimized Distributed Compute:** Tuning, configuring, and managing high-performance Spark clusters.

### 2. Snowflake + dbt (Data Build Tool)
High-growth product startups and analytics consulting firms (such as [Brillio](http://www.brillio.com/ "Brillio") or [MathCo](https://themathcompany.com/ "The Math Company")) are specifically demanding dbt proficiency for handling:
* **ELT Modular Workflows:** Writing clean, version-controlled, and testable SQL transformations.
* **In-Warehouse Execution:** Executing highly optimized transformations directly inside the Snowflake Data Cloud.

### 3. Cloud Infrastructure (AWS & Azure Dominating)
* **AWS Ecosystem:** Deep production knowledge of native ingestion and orchestration tools like **AWS Glue**, **Lambda**, **EMR**, and **S3**.
* **Azure Ecosystem:** High demand for modern analytics tracks including **Azure Data Factory (ADF)**, **Azure Synapse**, and **Microsoft Fabric** modernization roadmaps.
